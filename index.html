Pramish Paudel

I am a researcher at INSAIT, where I am fortunate to work with Prof. Luc Van Gool and Prof. Dr. Danda Pani Paudel.

Scientists found an ancient lake on Mars from an orbital image. The discovery was possible because the human mind can reason about cause and effect. My goal is to build world models capable of such causal reasoning, so that AI can make scientific discoveries too.

My UG thesis, iHuman (ECCV 2024), was the first top-tier vision paper from a Nepalese institute. I also co-founded sceneverse.ai/widelabs.ai, working on 3D and video understanding for clients like Pilatus Aircraft (demos).

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

  • iSEE: Object Permanence Through Self-Supervision

    Pramish Paudel, Ajad Chhatkuli, Luc Van Gool, Danda Pani Paudel

    Under review Paper Project Code Thread

    A self-supervised method that learns object permanence from video without labels; within 4.1 mAP of a supervised tracker on LA-CATER.

  • Vision Transformers Need Cross Aggregation for Patch Semantics (CALiT)

    Ajad Chhatkuli, Pramish Paudel, Deheng Zhang, Kanzhi Wu, Luc Van Gool, Danda Pani Paudel

    Under review Project

    A loss on the CLS token sends most ViT patches nearly the same gradient. Cross aggregation fixes this and sharpens patch features, for 0.6% extra FLOPs on ViT-S.

  • iHuman: Instant Animatable Digital Humans From Monocular Videos

    European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV) 2024 Paper Project Code Video
